# Windowsill (Paper Flowers) privacy policy

Windowsill is a small shared windowsill for a few close friends. This page says what the app keeps and why.

## What the app stores

| What | Why | Who can see it |
|---|---|---|
| The name you type | Shown under your pot | Friends on your windowsill |
| Your Sign in with Apple account ID, and the email Apple shares (often a private relay address) | So you can sign in again | No one else; it's only used for signing in |
| Your flowers: the petal shape you cut, the paper, petal count, centre, and when you planted it | To draw your flower for your friends | Friends on your windowsill |
| Which windowsill you're on | To show you the right friends | Friends on that windowsill |
| Gusts of wind on your windowsill | So everyone sees the same petals blow off | Friends on that windowsill |
| A push token for your widget | So your widget updates when a friend plants | No one else |

Your window colours are only saved on your own device.

## What the app doesn't do

- No ads, no tracking and no analytics.
- Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.
- No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

## Where it's kept

Everything listed above is stored with [Supabase](https://supabase.com), the app's database host. Widget updates go through Apple's push notification service.

## Deleting your account

In the app, open **settings → account & privacy → delete account**. This deletes your name, your flowers, your place on any windowsill and your widget token, and cancels the app's Sign in with Apple link. It can't be undone.

## Children

Windowsill isn't made for children under 13.
